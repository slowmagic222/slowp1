Two soft dynamic recordings at each central source note, plus a nearby-note soft recording for the third mapped variation. Third-source roots are 45, 52, 59, 65, 81 and 91, tuned using their actual recorded roots. These are not three same-note alternate takes. Far-bass source is replaced by root-45 soft recording, retaining its actual pitch metadata.
Quiet MIDI 59-65 region and third variations have offline constant gain balancing. Source attack/decay shapes remain. Complete natural openings retained, without attack splices or tail amplification.

0.7 preserves the source map and approximate opening level balance. Original audio is resampled offline, low-passed at 8.5 kHz, and given short boundary fades. See bank-report.json for per-recording gain and codec SNR.
