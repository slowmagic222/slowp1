# bows.msb: sources

All recordings are from **VSCO 2: Community Edition** by Versilian Studios (Sam Gossner), https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE, released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication). Versilian asks, not requires, that the samples are not sold on their own as a library and that credit goes to Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner with a link to the VSCO: CE homepage: this note is that credit.

Used (sustain with vibrato, the softest and loudest take of each note), by register:

- contrabass (Solo Contrabass/SusVib): F#1, G1, A#1 for notes up to B1
- cello section (Cello Section/susvib): C2, E2, G2, B2, D3 for C2 to E3
- viola section (Viola Section/susvib): E3, G3, B3, D4 for F3 to E4
- violin section (Violin Section/susVib): F#4, A4, C5, E5, G5, B5, D6 for F4 up

(VSCO's file names read an octave low: its "A2" sounds A3.) Mixed to mono, retuned to the note (the recordings sit up to 20 cents off), 32 kHz, 4.6 s from the bow, IMA ADPCM. `tools/vsco_to_msb.py` makes the bank from a VSCO-2-CE checkout.
