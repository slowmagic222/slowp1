Samples: Jeffrey Learman, 1977 Rhodes Mark I Stage 73.
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d/tree/aea5b8d3e11e2f7102593789a4e0a0e41b30271a
Samples CC BY-NC 4.0; retain SOURCE-LICENSE.txt. Original control files CC0.
Adaptations: mono, 12 kHz resampling, EQ, level matching, 1.75s stored attacks/tails, long ping-pong sustain and fitted decay, IMA ADPCM.
Three dynamic recordings per root; not same-dynamic alternate performances. Independent cycling per input note; no velocity selection.
Full pinned source files and hashes retained in ../sources and bank-report.json.

roads.msb / roads-rr.msb (Field, Nov 2026): the TINE 0.2 bank (tine-bank.bin, CRC 667688487) unchanged, put into an
MSB1 file by tools/tine_to_msb.py with TINE's loops, natural decay, source makeup and peaks. roads.msb lets velocity
choose the three dynamic recordings (soft 0..42, middle 43..84, hard 85..127); roads-rr.msb takes them in turn as the OG.
